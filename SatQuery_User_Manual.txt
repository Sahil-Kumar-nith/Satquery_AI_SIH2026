========================================================
SATQUERY AI — USER MANUAL (simple version)
========================================================
This guide explains how to use the app right now: what image
to upload, what to type, and what you should see back.
No technical knowledge needed.


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: WHAT IS THIS APP?
--------------------------------------------------------
SatQuery AI looks at satellite pictures and answers your
questions about them, in plain English.

There are two kinds of satellite pictures it understands:

  OPTICAL picture = a normal-looking photo from space
                     (like a photo of Earth, with colors)

  SAR picture      = a "radar" picture from space
                     (looks grainy/grey, but can see through
                     clouds, even at night)

You upload one or more pictures, then type a question, and
the app tells you what it found.


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: THE 4 UPLOAD BUTTONS — WHICH ONE DO I CLICK?
--------------------------------------------------------
At the bottom of the screen you will see 4 upload buttons:

  [ Optical ]   [ SAR ]   [ Before ]   [ After ]

Use this simple rule:

  -> Asking about ONE picture only?
     Upload it to "Optical" (normal photo) or "SAR" (radar photo).

  -> Asking "what changed" between TWO pictures of the
     SAME place at DIFFERENT times (like last year vs. this year)?
     Upload the older one to "Before" and the newer one to "After".

  -> Asking a question that needs BOTH an optical photo AND a
     radar (SAR) photo of the SAME place at the SAME time?
     Upload one to "Optical" and one to "SAR".

  -> Trying the special "cloudy picture" trick (see below)?
     Upload ONLY to "Optical" and "SAR" — make sure "Before" and
     "After" are empty/cleared, or the app will get confused
     about how many pictures you gave it.

IMPORTANT MISTAKE TO AVOID:
  Do NOT upload a single picture into "Before" if you just want
  to ask a normal question about it. "Before" only works together
  with "After". If you do this by mistake, the app will show a
  red error message telling you to move it to Optical/SAR instead.


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: WHAT FILES CAN I UPLOAD?
--------------------------------------------------------
Allowed file types: .tif  .tiff  .png  .jpg  .jpeg
Max file size: 200 MB

If your file is not a real satellite image (like a random
photo), the app may reject it — that's expected, it's checking
the file is a proper image.


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: WHAT CAN I ASK? (5 things the app can do today)
--------------------------------------------------------

There are 5 kinds of questions the app understands right now.
For each one below: which button(s) to upload to, an example
question you can type, and what you'll see back.

------------------------------------------------
(1) ASK ABOUT ONE PICTURE (VQA)
------------------------------------------------
Upload to:     Optical  (or SAR)
Example asks:  "What is the content of this image?"
               "Is there water in this image?"
               "What type of land is this?"

What you'll see:
  - A short text answer
  - A confidence label: HIGH / MEDIUM / INSUFFICIENT
    (this tells you how sure the app is — INSUFFICIENT means
    "don't fully trust this answer")

  SPECIAL TRICK: if your Optical picture is very cloudy, and you
  ALSO uploaded a SAR picture of the same place, the app will
  notice the clouds and automatically use the SAR picture
  instead — you'll see this happen in the trace under the
  answer. If it's cloudy and you have NO SAR picture, it will
  either still answer (but warn you not to fully trust it) or,
  if it's extremely cloudy, refuse to guess and ask you to
  upload a SAR picture instead. This is not a bug — it's the
  app being honest about what it can and can't see.

------------------------------------------------
(2) FIND SOMETHING IN ONE PICTURE (GROUNDING)
------------------------------------------------
Upload to:     Optical  (or SAR)
Example asks:  "Where is the water body?"
               "Highlight the buildings."
               "Locate the road."

What you'll see:
  - A yellow/green box drawn around the area it found
  - A confidence label: HIGH / MEDIUM / INSUFFICIENT
  - If nothing matches, it will honestly say "not found"
    instead of guessing
  - Same cloudy-picture trick as above: cloudy Optical + a SAR
    picture uploaded too = it automatically uses SAR instead.

------------------------------------------------
(3) COMPARE TWO PICTURES OF DIFFERENT DATES (CHANGE DETECTION)
------------------------------------------------
Upload to:     Before  AND  After  (two pictures, same place,
               different dates)
Example asks:  "What changed between these two images?"
               "Compare these two dates."

What you'll see:
  - A sentence describing how much changed (e.g. "11% of the
    area changed — HIGH change")
  - A box showing roughly where the biggest change happened

------------------------------------------------
(4) ASK A QUESTION ABOUT WHAT CHANGED (CHANGE-VQA)
------------------------------------------------
Upload to:     Before  AND  After
Example asks:  "Has the built-up area increased or decreased?"
               "Did this area flood between these two dates?"

What you'll see:
  - A plain-English answer to your specific question
  - The same "% changed" info as above, so you can double-check
    the answer makes sense

------------------------------------------------
(5) COMBINE A NORMAL PHOTO + RADAR PHOTO (OPTICAL + SAR)
------------------------------------------------
Upload to:     Optical  AND  SAR  (same place, same time)
Example asks:  "Use the optical and SAR images together to
               identify water and built-up areas."
               "Combine both sensors to find flooded areas."

What you'll see:
  - How much of the picture BOTH sensors agree is water/built-up
    (this is the most trustworthy number)
  - How much only the optical photo flagged
  - How much only the radar photo flagged
  - A confidence label: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: WHAT IS THE "EXECUTION TRACE"?
--------------------------------------------------------
Under every answer, you'll see a list like this:

  Query received: "..."
  Decision Trace: classified as ...
  Input validation: OK
  Tool selected: ...
  Tool Execution: running model...
  Evidence Trace: ...
  Evidence Fusion: ...

This is just the app showing its "work" step by step — like
showing your working in a math problem — so you can see WHY it
gave that answer, not just the answer itself. You don't need to
understand every line; it's there so nothing feels like magic.


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: HOW SURE SHOULD I BE ABOUT THE ANSWERS?
--------------------------------------------------------
This app is a hackathon PROTOTYPE, not a finished product.
Keep these things in mind:

  - HIGH / MEDIUM / INSUFFICIENT (or LOW) labels are the app's
    own best guess at how sure it is — not a lab-tested score.

  - Change detection and Optical+SAR fusion use simple math
    (comparing brightness/patterns), not a fully trained AI
    model yet. They work, but they're not perfect — they can
    be confused by shadows, clouds, or lighting differences.

  - If the app says "insufficient evidence" or "not found,"
    that is the honest answer — it means don't trust a guess
    here, not that something is broken.


--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: COMMON PROBLEMS AND WHAT THEY MEAN
--------------------------------------------------------

"role 'before' not valid ... expected one of ['single']"
  -> You uploaded to the wrong button. Move the picture to
     Optical or SAR instead of Before/After.

"Change tasks require exactly one 'before' image and one 'after' image."
  -> You need BOTH Before and After uploaded, not just one.

"Image could not be found on disk"
  -> The app was restarted and forgot your upload. Just
     re-upload the picture and ask again.

Red error box / "blocked"
  -> Not a crash — the app is telling you what's missing
     before it will run. Read the message, it tells you
     exactly what to fix.


--------------------------------------------------------
QUICK CHEAT SHEET
--------------------------------------------------------
Upload 1 picture (Optical or SAR)
  -> "What is this?"                     = plain answer
  -> "Where is the ___?"                 = box around it

Upload 2 pictures (Before + After)
  -> "What changed?"                     = change summary
  -> "Did ___ increase/decrease?"        = direct answer

Upload 2 pictures (Optical + SAR, same place/time)
  -> "Combine both sensors to find ___"  = agreement % + map
